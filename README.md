# Sales-analytics-project-excel

📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard
An interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard designed to provide a clear overview of sales performance, revenue, orders, customers, products, and regional trends. The dashboard transforms sales data into easy-to-understand visual insights to support business analysis and decision-making.

🚀 Dashboard Overview
The dashboard includes key performance indicators and multiple visualizations covering:

💰 Total Revenue: 14,43,358

📦 Total Quantity: 5,314

🧾 Total Orders: 500

💵 Average Order Value: 2,886.72

👥 Total Customers: 43

📈 Key Visualizations
The dashboard provides insights through:

Monthly Sales Trend – Tracks revenue performance across months.

Sales by Region – Compares sales across different regions.

Profit by Region – Visualizes regional profit distribution.

Top 5 Customers by Revenue – Highlights customers contributing the most revenue.

Sales vs Cost by Product – Compares product sales and associated costs.

Sales by Item Category – Shows the contribution of different product categories.

Quantity Sold by Product – Compares product-level quantities sold.

Orders Over Time – Displays monthly order volume trends.

🎛️ Interactive Filters
Users can filter the dashboard based on:

Month

Region

Item Category

These filters allow users to explore specific segments of the sales data and identify trends more easily.

🎯 Project Objectives
The main objectives of this project are to:

Monitor overall sales performance.

Identify high-performing products and categories.

Analyze regional sales and profitability.

Understand customer revenue contribution.

Track monthly sales and order trends.

Present business data through an intuitive visual dashboard.
